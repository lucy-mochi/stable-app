Everything you keep digging for, together.

Built for the person at the front desk or billing office who gets asked “what does Aetna pay for that?” and “when does that contract end?”

Every contract, organized

Group contracts by payer. Add amendments and exhibits as new versions, and keep the key terms (dates, notice periods, timely filing and appeal limits) one click away.

Never miss a renewal

Stable watches your dates and puts renewal notices, termination notices and expirations on your dashboard, with plenty of lead time.

Search the fine print

Find a clause across every contract in a second. Scanned pages are read automatically, right on your computer.

Fee schedules from messy files

Drop in the spreadsheet your payer sent. Stable finds the header row, suggests the columns, checks every row, and keeps a version for each effective date.

Compare payers by date

Look up a CPT or HCPCS code and see what each payer pays on any date of service. Contracts that pay a percent of Medicare are shown in dollars.

Private by design

Your data lives in a folder on your computer. Sign in with your own password, give teammates the access they need, and get an automatic daily backup.

First-time install help

Mac: open the downloaded file and drag Stable to Applications. The first time, right-click (or Control-click) Stable and choose Open, then Open again. Early-access builds aren't signed with a paid Apple certificate yet, so your Mac asks you to confirm.

Windows: run the installer. If Windows says it protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway. The same reason applies: it isn't signed with a paid certificate yet.

Linux: make the file executable (chmod +x Stable-linux-x64.AppImage) and run it.

Your contracts stay yours.

  • Everything is stored on your computer. Stable doesn't upload your documents anywhere.
  • Works fully offline, including reading scanned pages.
  • Passwords are stored scrambled, and repeated bad guesses lock the account for a while.
  • An automatic backup is saved about once a day, and you can download one any time.

Please keep patient information out.

Payer contracts and fee schedules don't normally contain patient information, and Stable is built for those documents. Don't upload anything that includes it. Because your data lives on your computer, your clinic's own protections (a password-protected computer and disk encryption) apply.

Questions

How much does it cost?

Stable is in early access, so it's free to try. We'll announce simple, flat pricing before charging anything, and early users will hear first.

Where is my data?

In a folder on your computer: ~/Library/Application Support/Stable on a Mac, %APPDATA%\Stable on Windows, and ~/.config/Stable on Linux. You can open it from the Help menu inside the app.

Can my whole team use it?

Yes, on one computer: add teammates in Settings and each person signs in with their own password and role (admin, billing or read-only). Syncing between several computers isn't available yet.

How do I move to a new computer?

In Settings, download a backup. On the new computer, choose Restore from a backup instead the first time you open Stable.

Can it read scanned contracts?

Yes. Scanned PDFs and photos (PNG or JPEG) are read automatically in the background, in English, and then become searchable. Word files work too.

What kinds of fee schedule files work?

CSV and Excel (.xlsx) files, including ones with title rows, several sheets or oddly named columns. You confirm the columns before anything is saved, and Stable lists any rows it skipped.

Is it HIPAA compliant?

Stable is made for payer contracts and fee schedules, which normally don't contain patient information, and it asks you not to upload any. It doesn't send your data anywhere. We don't make compliance claims for your clinic; your own safeguards still apply.

Why does my computer warn me when I open it?

Early-access builds aren't signed with paid developer certificates yet. See the install help above for the two extra clicks.
